Cancellation & Refund Policy

This Cancellation & Refund Policy applies to all payments made to Prismabit Digital Solution LLP (“Company”, “we”, “our”, “us”) through our website or via payment gateways such as Cashfree.

1. Cancellation Policy

  • Service cancellations must be requested in writing via email.
  • Cancellations are subject to the terms mentioned in the project proposal, invoice, or service agreement.
  • Once project execution or service delivery has started, cancellation may not be permitted.
  • Advance or milestone payments are generally non-cancellable unless explicitly agreed in writing.

2. Refund Policy

  • Refunds are applicable only if clearly mentioned in a written agreement or proposal.
  • No refunds will be provided for completed milestones, delivered services, or consumed resources.
  • Payment gateway charges, transaction fees, and applicable taxes are non-refundable.
  • Refund eligibility is evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

3. Refund Processing

If a refund is approved, it will be processed through the original mode of payment within 7–10 business days. The actual credit time may vary depending on the payment gateway or bank.

4. Non-Refundable Scenarios

  • Change of mind after payment
  • Delay caused due to incomplete or incorrect information from the client
  • Third-party service costs already incurred
  • Digital services already delivered or activated

5. Disputes

Any disputes related to cancellations or refunds shall be addressed amicably. If unresolved, disputes shall be subject to the jurisdiction of courts in India.
